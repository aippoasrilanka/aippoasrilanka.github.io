President's Message

Leadership with unity, knowledge and service

A message from the President of AIPPOA Sri Lanka.

From the President

Welcome to AIPPOA Sri Lanka

It is my great pleasure to welcome you to the official website of the All Island Private Pharmacy Owners Association of Sri Lanka (AIPPOA). Our association is committed to bringing together private pharmacy owners across Sri Lanka and strengthening our profession through unity, knowledge, professionalism and service.

Through education, professional development, community service and collaboration with relevant stakeholders, we strive to create greater opportunities for our members while contributing positively to the health and wellbeing of the people of Sri Lanka.

I invite pharmacy owners across the country to join us in building a strong, united, knowledgeable and responsible pharmacy community. Together, through unity and service, let us contribute towards a healthier Sri Lanka.

Deshamanya Chandika Gankanda (JP)
President — All Island Private Pharmacy Owners Association (AIPPOA), Sri Lanka
With best wishes,
AIPPOA Sri Lanka
