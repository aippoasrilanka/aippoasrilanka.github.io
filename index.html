About AIPPOA Sri Lanka

Building a connected, knowledgeable and responsible private pharmacy community across Sri Lanka.

Our Vision

To strengthen the private pharmacy sector through unity, professional development, education, responsible practice and meaningful community service.

  • Represent and connect private pharmacy owners.
  • Promote continuous education and knowledge sharing.
  • Support professional standards and responsible pharmacy practice.
  • Contribute to community health and social service.

Unity • Knowledge • Service

AIPPOA provides a platform for pharmacy owners to work together, exchange experience, participate in educational programmes and support communities throughout Sri Lanka.

Our association grows stronger when every district works together.
